About

I am an undergraduate student at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, studying Data Science and Big Data Technology with Contemporary Entrepreneurialism. My interests sit at the intersection of data systems and network behaviour: how information is encoded, transported, measured, and explained.

Recent coursework has involved building TCP and UDP video streaming clients in Python and analysing the resulting packet captures with Wireshark. I prefer work that can be verified: reproducible tests, explicit measurement definitions, and conclusions that separate direct observation from inference.

Selected work

Coursework projects that connect implementation with evidence: what was built, what was measured, and what the results do and do not show.

TCP / UDP video streaming

Built two socket-based video streaming pipelines in Python and compared them across 1080p and 640×360 workloads. The experiment measured frame completion, latency, throughput, packetisation, and retransmission behaviour; reconstructed outputs were checked by frame count and MD5 equality.
